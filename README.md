# AgriMarket AI – Smart Agro-Supply Chain Platform

AgriMarket AI is an AI-driven platform designed to revolutionize the agricultural supply chain in East Africa. It connects smallholder farmers directly to buyers, provides real-time market data, and optimizes logistics, reducing post-harvest losses and improving farmer incomes.

## 🚀 Competitive Advantage
AgriMarket AI uniquely combines market access, AI-based price prediction, and smart logistics in one integrated platform, tailored specifically for rural smallholder farmers. Unlike fragmented competitors, we serve the full agri-value chain from produce to payment.
